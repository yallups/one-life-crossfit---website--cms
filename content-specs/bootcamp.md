## Program Details / Offering
- Bootcamp is CrossFit - Simplified
  - These are coach-led CrossFit conditioning classes that focus on longer, continuous workouts using accessible movements. Bootcamp removes the complexity of Olympic lifting while keeping the effectiveness, coaching, and intensity that CrossFit is known for.
  - Instead of splitting the class into separate strength and conditioning pieces, Bootcamp typically features one longer workout (20–30 minutes) that challenges your cardiovascular fitness, muscular endurance, and mental toughness.
  - Expect functional movements at a higher pace, with less technical skill required.

- Bootcamp is for EVERYONE
  - Are new to CrossFit and want a less complicated entry point
  - Want to focus on weight loss and conditioning
  - Feel intimidated by barbell or Olympic lifting
  - Are returning from injury or want lower-skill training days
  - Want a challenging workout without complex movements
  - Bootcamp is for athletes who want conditioning-focused training without full CrossFit class movements.
  - Want to add extra metabolic conditioning alongside CrossFit

## Class Format / What To Expect
- How it works
  1. Warm-Up & Movement Prep — Get ready to move and raise your heart rate.
  2. Coach-Led Workout — A continuous, high-energy workout using accessible movements.
  3. Cool Down & Recovery — Lower the heart rate and reset.
  - Class length is 45 minutes.
  - Coaches provide scaling and coaching cues for every fitness level.

## FAQs
- Q: Is Bootcamp good for beginners?
  A: Yes. Bootcamp uses simpler movement patterns and is a great starting point for anyone new to fitness or [CrossFit](/programs/group-training/crossfit).
- Q: How is Bootcamp different from CrossFit?
  A: Bootcamp keeps training functional and high intensity, but removes complex lifts and focuses on continuous conditioning.
- Q: How long is class?
  A: Bootcamp classes are 45 minutes.
- Q: Can I do Bootcamp and CrossFit?
  A: Absolutely. Many members use Bootcamp as extra conditioning alongside their [CrossFit](/programs/group-training/crossfit) training.
- Q: Will I still get coaching and scaling?
  A: Yes. Every class is coach-led with scaling options to match your ability and comfort.
- Q: Is Bootcamp good for weight loss?
  A: Bootcamp is designed for high-intensity conditioning, making it an excellent option for weight loss and improved fitness.
- Q: What if I have injuries or limitations?
  A: Coaches can scale movements for injuries, limitations, or low-impact needs.
