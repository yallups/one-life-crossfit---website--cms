## FAQs

- Q: What is an InBody scan?
  A: A fast, non‑invasive body composition scan that measures lean mass, body fat, and hydration.
- Q: How often should I scan?
  A: Most people scan every 4–8 weeks to track trends without overreacting to daily fluctuations.
- Q: Are InBody scans free?
  A: You can purchase scans in the gym, and you get a free weigh‑in with any private training or nutrition coaching session.
