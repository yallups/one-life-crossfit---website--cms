## FAQs

- Q: How many people can be in a semi-private group?
  A: Typically 2–4 people per coach, depending on goals and scheduling.
- Q: Can partners have different goals?
  A: Yes. Sessions are tailored to each person’s needs even if goals differ.
- Q: Do we need the same schedule?
  A: You’ll book sessions together, so aligned schedules work best.
- Q: Can we add nutrition coaching?
  A: Yes. We can include nutrition/lifestyle coaching if desired.
