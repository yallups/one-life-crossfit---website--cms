## Class Format / What To Expect
- How it works
  1. Warm-Up & Movement Preparation — Prepare your body and review movements for the day.
  2. Strength or Skill Training — Build strength, technique, and confidence through coached progressions.
  3. Conditioning Workout — A challenging but scalable workout designed for fat loss and fitness.
  4. Cool Down & Recovery — Finish strong and support long-term progress.
  - Most classes have 10–18 members, capped at 20. Saturday classes may be larger for special workouts or events. There is always a coach present, actively guiding, correcting, and modifying workouts to meet each athlete’s needs.
  - Every workout has one goal—but how you reach it is personalized to you.
- New members start with a clear onboarding process to build confidence and safety from day one.
- Fundamentals includes 1:1 or small-group coaching, education on CrossFit methodology, and scaling prescriptions so you feel confident in class.
  - Experienced athletes can train with RX+ options, specialty cycles, and advanced coaching.

## FAQs
- Q: I’m new to CrossFit. Can I still join?
  A: Yes. We have a dedicated onboarding process that teaches you the movements, how to scale, and how to train safely so you can build confidence quickly.
- Q: What if I have injuries or limitations?
  A: Our coaches are trained to scale workouts for injuries, mobility restrictions, and all fitness levels. We’ll personalize your options each class.
- Q: Do you offer advanced options for experienced athletes?
  A: Yes. We provide RX and RX+ options, specialty training cycles, and progression work for athletes who want to push performance.
- Q: How big are the classes?
  A: Most classes have 10–18 members and are capped at 20. You’ll always have a coach actively instructing, correcting, and modifying.
- Q: How often should I attend?
  A: Most members see the best results with 3–5 classes per week. We’ll help you choose a schedule that matches your recovery and goals.
- Q: What is a typical class like?
  A: Every class includes a warm-up, strength or skill work, a conditioning workout, and a cool down—plus coaching throughout.
- Q: Do you offer specialty programs?
  A: Yes. We run specialty programs throughout the year to help members focus on strength, endurance, or performance goals.
- Q: What if I want help with nutrition or weight loss?
  A: We can pair your training with nutrition coaching for faster, sustainable results.
