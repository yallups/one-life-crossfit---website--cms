# Brand Voice

## Vibe adjectives
- Fun
- Kinda savage
- Approachable
- Strong
- Fast results

## Tone
- Straightforward
- Confident
- Coach-led
- High-energy without hype

## Era/style influences
- 90s / early 2000s attitude
- 2013–2018 CrossFit Games style language
- Warped Tour energy

## Guardrails
- Do not use cuss words
- Avoid medical or guaranteed outcome claims
- Emphasize confidence and capability without arrogance

## Messaging pillars
- We are strong, fit, and competitive—without being unwelcoming
- Training is efficient, results-focused, and coach-guided
- The gym feels like a crew: gritty, fun, and supportive

## Sample language patterns
- Short, punchy sentences
- Active verbs and direct calls to action
- Slight edge, but still friendly

## Example voice snippets (internal)
- Train hard. Stay humble. Get results.
- Serious coaching. Fun crew. Fast progress.
- Old‑school grit, modern coaching.
