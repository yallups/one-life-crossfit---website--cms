# Intake Questions (CrossFit Service Content)

## Essential basics
- What is the service/program name?
- Who is it for (fitness level, age, goals, schedule constraints)?
- What problem does it solve for members?
- What makes your gym’s approach unique vs. other CrossFit gyms?
- What is the primary CTA (book intro, free trial, schedule consult, join now)?

## Program details
- Class format and length (e.g., 60 min class: warmup, skill, metcon, cooldown)
- Weekly frequency / schedule cadence
- Coach-to-member ratio or class size cap
- Required experience or onboarding steps
- Equipment or facility highlights

## Membership & pricing
- Membership tiers and what each includes
- Pricing range or starting price
- Commitment terms, cancellation policy, trial offers
- What’s included: nutrition guidance, goal reviews, open gym, specialty classes

## Proof points
- Coach credentials/certifications
- Member results stories or testimonials
- Any notable events, community initiatives, or competitions

## Brand voice
- 3–5 adjectives that describe your gym’s vibe
- Tone preference (straightforward, energetic, premium, family-friendly)
- Words to avoid
