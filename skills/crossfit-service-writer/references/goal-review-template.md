## **💊 Prescriptive Model Update for One Life CrossFit**

### **🧭 Philosophy**

Every new client gets a **prescription**—not a price list.  
Your job is to identify their goals and prescribe the *minimum effective dose* of training, nutrition, and accountability to achieve those goals.

After Discovery, you pivot to:

“Based on what you’ve told me, here’s what I recommend for you.”

Then show **ONE** clear option that fits their needs, followed by **scaled-down alternatives** if cost or schedule becomes the main objection.

## **Mission:**
