- Baseline private training session for benchmarks

## Prescriptive model (consultation philosophy)
- Discover goals, barriers, and lifestyle first.
- Prescribe the minimum effective dose of training, nutrition, and accountability.
- Offer one clear best-fit option; show scaled-down alternatives if needed.
- Set a start date, collect payment, book the first appointment immediately.
